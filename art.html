About the artist:

Xiolet Woolfy

Hello! I'm Xiolet, a 20 year old cartoon artist from the US, and the owner of WoolfyWorks since 2018. Some of the big inspirations behind my art are anime, nature, and other creatives in the Furry Fandom! I am currently studying Game Design and 3D modeling at University, while running my Etsy Shop and often taking general art commissions through social media. I also set up booths and vend at local art markets whenever I get the opportunity! Some of my art passions include 2D and digital art, sculpting, costume, cosplay, live 2D rigging, merchandise design/production and a lot more! Outside of art I enjoy nature and anime. I am dedicated strongly to quality and customer service under the WoolfyWorks brand, and I am always excited to talk with customers about bringing their creative ideas to life!
